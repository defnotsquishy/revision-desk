# Key Sociologists — AQA GCSE Sociology 8192

Added 5 November 2026. The separate `soc-sociologists` deck adds 60 original explanatory flashcards and 30 multiple-choice questions. The six existing Sociology decks and their saved-rating IDs are retained.

## Scope

The coverage inventory is the 25 named text/study headings in [AQA Appendix B](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/sociology/gcse/sociology-8192/specification/appendix-b-texts-and-summaries). There are 32 distinct authors when joint authors are counted and repeated authors are counted once. This is a defined GCSE revision list, not every sociologist ever mentioned in a textbook or every author cited incidentally by those texts.

- Families: Delphy and Leonard; Oakley; Parsons; Robert and Rhona Rapoport; Young and Willmott; Zaretsky.
- Education: Ball; Ball, Bowe and Gewirtz; Bowles and Gintis; Durkheim; Halsey, Heath and Ridge; Parsons; Willis.
- Crime and deviance: Becker; Carlen; Albert Cohen; Heidensohn; Merton.
- Social stratification: Davis and Moore; Devine; Marx; Murray; Townsend; Walby; Weber.

The core topics are cross-checked with AQA's [Families](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/sociology/gcse/sociology-8192/specification/subject-content/families), [Education](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/sociology/gcse/sociology-8192/specification/subject-content/education), [Crime and deviance](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/sociology/gcse/sociology-8192/specification/subject-content/crime-and-deviance) and [Social stratification](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/sociology/gcse/sociology-8192/specification/subject-content/social-stratification) sections. These links set curriculum scope; the wording and application examples are newly authored.

## Explanatory material

The student's supplied `1.Revision Sheets - Sociology.pptx`, its PDF version and the Families, Education, Crime and Deviance and Social Stratification knowledge organisers provide the underlying revision material. Their previously extracted text is in the private `work/sociology-extracted` workspace, outside the published website. The public app contains original question/answer explanations, not scans or reproductions of those sheets or published textbooks.

Each named text has a key-idea card and an evaluation card. Ten further cards practise distinctions, comparisons and applications; five also have a multiple-choice version. The 25 overview quizzes use nearby theories from the same subject area as distractors. Correct option zero is shuffled by the established app, and the full explanation is shown after answering.

Care is taken to distinguish Parsons's two texts and Ball's two studies, Oakley's conventional-family text from her housework research, Albert from Stanley Cohen, and revisiting a locality from following the same panel. Murray's views and other theories are attributed claims to evaluate. Historical evidence is dated; it is not presented as current population data. Evaluation and exam-use prompts are teaching guidance, not quotations from an AQA mark scheme or a guarantee of marks.

The existing Flashcards/Multiple choice selector, topic filter, account ratings and print dialog handle the new deck. Stable new IDs avoid changing confidence on older cards. The app's catalogue totals are calculated from the dataset.
