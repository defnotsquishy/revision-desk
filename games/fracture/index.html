An original battleground

Direction is everything.

FRACTURE is built around quick movement, readable attacks and a four-hit melee chain. Start in Meridian’s training plaza: move, dash and use gravity to control the dummy and break marked props.

VECTOR

An original directional-force fighter. Four base abilities, Vector Shift and a temporary awakening set.

Training first

Learn your range and timings. Damage, hits, combo damage and DPS come from your actual attacks, not an invented score.

Development notes

The first arena

Version 0.1 is the local training vertical slice. This is not yet an online multiplayer release. Public matchmaking, private codes and additional original characters remain part of the full goal.

Bloom and motion blur are not implemented in this version. Low graphics reduces resolution, shadows and particle effects for weaker PCs. Your school or browser may restrict WebGL or mouse capture; this app does not bypass those restrictions.

VECTOR

Gravity and directional force. Short-range pressure with space to reposition.

  1. Gravity Strike · A heavy, close-range punch.
  2. Repulse · A forward burst that launches your target.
  3. Gravity Well · Pull nearby targets into a temporary field.
  4. Zero Point · A close-range gravity blow with stronger knockback.

R Vector Shift · Reposition in your movement direction.

G Awakening · Earn energy by fighting, then use four temporary enhanced abilities.

More original characters are planned. There’s no character switch to pretend they’re playable yet.

WASD movement · Mouse camera · Left click basic attack · Space jump · Shift sprint · Q dash · Hold F to block · R Vector Shift · 1–4 abilities · G awakening · Escape menu.

Face your target and stay in range. The fourth basic hit launches the dummy. Heavy attacks can break marked props. Click the arena for mouse capture, or drag to look if capture is unavailable.
