    About your revision desk

    Privacy & your data

    Technical information checked 3 October 2026.

    Privacy (draft) Cookies & storage Using the app Copyright Source & licences

    Draft notice — operator review needed

    The storage details below describe the current app. A complete privacy notice still needs the operator’s identity and private contact route, confirmed legal basis, retention policy and international-transfer arrangements. These have not been confirmed. This draft is not a claim of UK GDPR compliance.

    The short version

    You can revise as a guest. Signing in lets you save your profile and flashcard progress to your account. Papers you import, drawings, whiteboards and typed practice notes stay in this browser on this device; the app does not upload them to Firebase.

    Use a nickname and a picture you are happy to save. You do not need to provide your school, address, date of birth or location. Do not put sensitive personal information in your notes or profile. The app has no advertising or analytics integration.

    What is saved, and why

    Account details
    Firebase Authentication handles your email, password credentials, account identifier, verification status and sign-in session. These let you sign in, verify your email and reset your password. The app does not store your password in profile or progress documents.
    Profile
    Your chosen display name, small profile picture, badge and accent colour let you personalise the desk. A picture is cropped and re-encoded as a 256px JPEG, removing its original metadata. A guest profile stays locally; saving a signed-in profile sends it to Firestore.
    Study progress
    Card ratings, deck identifiers, dated daily “Know” counts and save timestamps let you resume revision and see your graph. Signed-in progress saves to Firestore; guest progress stays locally. Guest ratings are uploaded only if you choose Import guest ratings and confirm.
    Practice files
    Imported PDF bytes, ink, whiteboards, typed notes and document identifiers are stored in this device’s IndexedDB database to resume your work. Account-scoped drawings still do not follow you to another device.
    Preferences and session cache
    Browser storage remembers appearance and study mode, and holds a signed-in account’s temporary cache and pending rating changes. Read Cookies & storage for the details and appearance storage choice.

    Who provides the services

    The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub says it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, including when they are not signed into GitHub. See GitHub’s hosting information and GitHub’s privacy statement.

    Google Firebase provides account authentication and the cloud database. The account service loads from Google when the online app starts, including for guests, so those network requests expose connection details such as your IP address to the provider. Guest ratings and guest profiles are not automatically uploaded.

    The Firestore database is configured in London, UK (europe-west2). This does not mean all account processing stays in the UK: Firebase states that Authentication processes data in the United States. See Firebase privacy and processing locations. The operator must confirm the applicable transfer safeguards before finalising this notice.

    Cloud rules separate users by their authenticated account ID. Other ordinary accounts cannot read your profile or progress. Authorised project administrators and service providers can have administrative access; the app is not end-to-end encrypted.

    Opening or fetching an official paper contacts the examination board’s server, which can receive connection information. External links follow the destination’s policies. Exported backups are files on your computer; look after them, especially on shared devices.

    How long information stays

    Currently, saved cloud records have no automatic expiry in the app. Signing out does not delete your cloud data. Reset deck changes ratings but keeps earlier graph history and does not delete your profile or account. There is currently no in-app account-deletion control. The operator still needs to confirm a retention and deletion process.

    Guest progress, profiles, appearance and practice documents remain until removed, browser site data is cleared, or the browser evicts them. Account session caches and unconfirmed changes are temporary. Wait for “Saved to your account” before closing; pending edits can be lost when a browser session ends. Downloaded exports remain until you remove them yourself.

    Your choices and privacy requests

    You can use guest mode instead of creating an account, edit your profile, remove your picture and save, reset deck ratings, sign out, and export drawing backups. Clearing browser data removes local work, not server-side account records. Export practice files first.

    Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection rights include access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. The operator’s private contact for making these requests has not yet been supplied. Do not post your email, passwords, account details or personal data in public GitHub issues.

    Right to object: you may be able to object to processing depending on its legal basis. The basis and private request route must be confirmed by the operator. The appearance storage choice is available under Cookies & storage.

    You can read about your rights at the ICO or make a privacy complaint to the ICO. If this notice is confusing, ask a trusted adult to help you read it.

    No exam-grade decisions

    Confidence ratings, graph points and badges summarise your practice. They are not official grades, and the app does not make admission, grading or other legal decisions about you.

    Browser storage, not just cookies

    The app does not set its own cookies using document.cookie. It does use local storage, session storage and IndexedDB. These also store information on your device. There are no advertising pixels, analytics SDKs or marketing-cookie controls in this version.

    Your appearance storage choice

    One small local entry remembers this on/off choice. You can change it here at any time. Customise Desk controls the appearance itself.

    Appearance and study mode · local storage
    revision-desk-appearance-v1 remembers theme, density, text size, panel corners, question font, reading spacing, contrast, motion and shortcuts; legacy revision-desk-theme and revision-desk-mode remember earlier choices. revision-desk-remember-appearance-v1 remembers your storage choice. These device preferences do not sync to Firebase. They have no automatic expiry; turning remembering off removes the preference values.
    Guest revision · local storage
    revision-desk-progress-v2, revision-desk-history-v1 and revision-desk-profile-v1:guest keep guest ratings, dated graph points and your guest profile between visits. They are device-wide guest data, not an account login. They stay until removed or site storage is cleared or evicted.
    Signed-in cache · session storage
    revision-desk-account-session-v1:{account ID} temporarily caches your account data and pending rating edits. Firebase Authentication also uses session persistence for sign-in. These are for the browser session; session restoration can depend on your browser. Confirmed cloud records remain separately in Firebase.
    Paper practice · IndexedDB
    revision-desk-practice-v1 keeps PDFs, ink, whiteboards and typed notes on this device, separated by guest/account ID. It has no automatic expiry and is not a cloud backup.
    Older game version · local storage
    If you used the removed FRACTURE game, fracture-settings-v1 and fracture-library-v1 may still contain its device-only settings and recent launch. The current app no longer reads or writes them. Removing the game did not clear your browser data or revision work. Browser site-data controls can remove these older entries; export any device-only revision work before clearing all site data.
    Canyon Run · local storage
    The separate driving page remembers only your best lap in canyon-run-best-v1. It stays in this browser, has no automatic expiry, and does not sync to your revision account. Revision pages do not load the game or read this entry. Browser site-data controls can remove it; export any device-only revision work before clearing all site data.

    Clearing or blocking storage

    Your browser’s site-data controls can remove or block local storage. Removing site data can permanently lose device-only papers and drawings, so export them first. Blocking storage can prevent saved revision or sign-in from working. Signing out is not a substitute for removing local files on a shared computer.

    If the app changes

    This notice must be reviewed before adding tracking or a new storage purpose. A decorative “Accept all” button is not proof of consent or compliance. The ICO’s storage and access guidance covers cookies and other device storage.

    Revision help, not an examination service

    Revision Desk is an independent study tool. It is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by AQA, Pearson Edexcel, your school or the authors of the texts you study.

    Flashcards and explanations support your class notes. Interpretations can differ, and errors are possible. Check the current specification, your teacher’s guidance and the matching official mark scheme. Practising a topic does not predict an exam question or guarantee a grade.

    Past papers and uploads

    Choose the correct board, course, tier, component and series. Older papers may use different assessment arrangements. The catalogue contains located public resources, not every paper ever issued. Do not upload or redistribute secure, unreleased or unauthorised materials. Upload only files and pictures you have the right to use.

    Board papers and mark schemes open from the original publisher. An imported PDF is not automatically matched to a mark scheme. Drawings do not change the original paper. A whiteboard or exported written paper is your practice, not a marked examination submission.

    Look after your account and work

    Use your own account and a unique password. Do not try to access someone else’s data or disrupt the service. Only import guest ratings you made yourself. On a shared device, sign out and take care of any saved local practice files.

    Wait for a confirmed cloud save before closing. Export important drawings and notes: they are device-only and browser storage can be lost. Services, quotas and third-party links can be unavailable; uninterrupted availability is not promised.

    Feedback and rights

    Report non-sensitive bugs or incorrect study content through the public GitHub issue tracker. Do not include personal account information there. This guidance does not remove any rights you have under applicable law.
