KART ROYALE - unofficial fan experiment
=======================================

A 3D kart racer that runs in the web browser: 12 racers, 3 courses, 10 items.
Not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo or LeBron James.

HOW TO PLAY
Open index.html through a web host (it has to be served over http/https;
double-clicking the file on your own computer won't load the game).
Best in an up-to-date Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a computer with WebGL 2.

CONTROLS (keyboard / controller)
  Steer ............ A / D or Left / Right      | left stick
  Accelerate ....... W or Up                    | A or RT
  Brake / reverse .. S or Down                  | B or LT
  Hop + drift ...... Space or Shift (hold)      | RB or X
  Use item ......... E or K (hold S/Down to aim back) | LB
  Look behind ...... Q
  Reset camera ..... C                          | Y
  Pause ............ Esc or P                   | Start
  Menus ............ Arrows, Enter / Space, Backspace

Tips: hold accelerate just after "2" for a rocket start. Hold a drift longer for
blue -> orange -> purple sparks, then let go for a boost. The further back you
are, the better (and more) items you get.
