The game runs in an isolated frame with no revision-account connection. Races and game settings aren’t cloud-synced; settings reset when you reopen it. Restarting leaves the current race.

Keyboard controls & tips
Drive
W / ↑ to accelerate · A / D or ← / → to steer · S / ↓ to brake
Drift
Hold Space or Shift, then release for a boost
Item
E or K · hold S / ↓ to aim backwards
Camera
Q to look behind · C to reset
Pause & menus
Esc or P to pause · arrows and Enter to choose · Backspace to go back

Click inside the game first to give it keyboard focus. Fullscreen uses the whole player; Escape leaves fullscreen. Accelerate just after “2” for a rocket start.
