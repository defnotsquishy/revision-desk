## 1st Class Maths

All 18 Maths links were extracted from the provider's [Edexcel GCSE revision table](https://www.1stclassmaths.com/edexcelrevision), not guessed from search queries. YouTube oEmbed returned each expected lesson title and the author **1st Class Maths**, with [the official channel](https://www.youtube.com/@1stClassMaths). Number, algebra, ratio/proportion, geometry, probability and statistics are grouped independently of science.

Quadratic formula, direct/inverse proportionality equations, circle theorems, conditional probability and histograms retain the provider's explicit Higher labels. Other videos are described simply as GCSE lessons, not guaranteed Foundation-only. Tree diagrams carry an extensions note. The collection links general GCSE topics from an Edexcel directory; only Sets & Venn Diagrams explicitly names Edexcel in the video title. [Pearson content guidance](https://qualifications.pearson.com/content/dam/pdf/GCSE/mathematics/2015/teaching-and-learning-materials/GCSE_%289-1%29_Mathematics_Content_Guidance.pdf) supports tier distinctions. No paper-specific predictions are made.

Metadata checks confirm public resources and author identity, not complete playback, region availability or accessibility quality. YouTube and provider sites control their own account/cookie/storage behaviour when a student follows a link. Visiting is optional and no revision account data is supplied to them by the app.
