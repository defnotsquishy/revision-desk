## 1st Class Maths

The canonical maths-resources-data.js directory records every current row of the provider's [Edexcel GCSE revision table](https://www.1stclassmaths.com/edexcelrevision): **106 topics**, each with an explanation video, question PDF and solution PDF, plus the Ultimate Foundation and Higher revision booklets. The [Level 2 Further Maths table](https://www.1stclassmaths.com/l2-further-maths) contributes **23 topics**, each with an explanation video, question PDF and separate video solution, plus its combined booklet. Desktop/mobile duplicates were removed by exact topic identity; no video IDs or PDF hashes were guessed. Home derives its Maths/Further Maths explanation lists from this same dataset, rather than maintaining a competing small catalogue. The original 18 selected Maths links remain solely as a fallback if the full public asset fails to load.

GCSE resources are grouped into Number, Algebra & graphs, Ratio & proportion, Geometry & measures, Probability and Statistics. Further Maths has separate Number, Algebra, Functions, Coordinate geometry, Differentiation, Matrices and Trigonometry sections. This grouping is Revision Deck navigation, not copied provider section headings. [AQA 8365](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/mathematics/aqa-certificate/mathematics-8365/specification) is an untiered Level 2 qualification, not A-level Further Maths or an extra GCSE tier.

The provider's difficulty images were visually checked and replaced with accessible text labels 1+ through 8+. Grade 6+–8+ resources receive Higher guidance; lower labels cover shared/basic work and are not guaranteed Foundation-only. Videos or complete worksheets may include harder extensions. These are starting-difficulty labels, not promised grades or exam predictions. Historical appearance percentages are omitted: their old cutoff and grouped-topic caveats do not support current or future probability claims.
