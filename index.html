      Your account

      Sign in to keep your profile, picture, ratings and daily graph in private Firebase storage, separate from other people’s accounts. Guest progress is never imported automatically. Closing the browser signs you out.

      Connecting to account service…

      Only import ratings you made yourself. Unsaved cloud changes are kept for this browser session, not permanently; wait for “Saved to your account” before closing.

      Use a unique password. New accounts need at least 8 characters.

      Changes haven’t reached your account yet

      If you sign out now, queued ratings stay only in this browser session. Sign back into the same account in this window to retry. Closing the window can lose unsaved changes.

      Import your guest ratings?

      This device’s guest ratings may include other people’s practice. Import only if these are yours. This adds missing ratings to your signed-in account; existing account ratings, guest ratings, profiles and previous graph dates are unchanged. New graph points use today’s date.
